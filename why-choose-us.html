Why Choose Us

We combine engineering experience, construction capability, skilled personnel and practical project delivery to support clients across marine, civil and infrastructure projects.

01

On-Schedule, On-Budget Delivery

Completion and delivery of projects in line with client requirements, on schedule and within budget, while protecting the client's interests.

02

Safety & Environmental Commitment

Commitment to achieving zero injuries, protecting the environment and contributing to the development of host communities.

03

Unity of Purpose

A unity of purpose between the customer and the company on every project.

04

Experienced Personnel

Experienced engineering and construction personnel, including civil/structural engineers, designers, construction specialists and experienced crews.

05

Specialized Equipment

Access to specialized construction and marine equipment supporting piling, dredging, jetty, road and bridge works.

Quality & HSE Integration

Quality assurance and health, safety and environmental practices integrated into project delivery.

Quality Workmanship, Safety and Timely Delivery

From project planning to delivery, Patigaa Nigeria Limited focuses on quality workmanship, safety, responsible environmental practices, timely delivery and alignment with client requirements.
