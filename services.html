Our Services

Patigaa Nigeria Limited provides engineering, construction, management and technical support services for marine, civil, structural and infrastructure projects.

Service 01

Piling & Foundations

Piling and foundation works using specialized piling equipment, hydraulic vibratory hammers, diesel hammers, pile clamps and experienced piling crews. Capabilities include concrete sheet piles, casing piles and steel sheet piles.

  • Hydraulic vibratory and diesel pile hammers
  • Concrete, casing and steel sheet piling
  • Experienced piling crews and pile clamps
View related equipment

Service 02

Jetty Construction

Construction of marine jetties using steel sheet piles or reinforced concrete sheet piles, supported by modern piling equipment, experienced crews and locally or internationally sourced materials.

See jetty projects

Service 03

Road Construction

Clearing, scarification and filling, base-course preparation using stone dust, asphalt works and related civil infrastructure activities.

See road projects

Service 04

Bridge Construction

Subsoil investigation, soil testing and design to determine piling depth, concrete strength and structural requirements, together with bridge-related precast and construction works.

Service 05

Dredging & Reclamation

Swamp, sand stockpiling and reclamation dredging services within Nigeria, including sweeping, stockpiling and reclamation works.

Construction

Building, Marine & Site Infrastructure

Additional construction capability supporting our core piling, jetty, road and dredging services.

Service 06

Building & Estate Works

Building, housing and estate construction services supported by civil/structural engineering and construction expertise.

Service 07

Landing Aprons, Ramps & Slipways

Construction support for landing aprons, vessel landing platforms, ramps and slipways for marine and coastal projects.

Service 08

Fabrication & Workshop Works

Fabrication and workshop construction activities as part of our wider engineering and construction scope.

Service 09

Tanks, Drainage, Power Lines & Pipelines

Construction of tanks, drainage systems and fences, plus infrastructure support covering power lines, site facilities and pipelines.

Service 10

Procurement & Supply

Procurement and supply support for engineering and construction activities.

Service 11

Maintenance Services

Engineering and construction maintenance services supporting the continued operation of infrastructure and facilities.

Service 12

Equipment Hiring & Leasing

Equipment hiring and leasing services to support construction and engineering operations.
