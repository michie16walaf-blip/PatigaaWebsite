About Patigaa Nigeria Limited

Built on Engineering Discipline

Patigaa Nigeria Limited is an independent consulting and construction company owned by a group of distinguished Nigerians with expertise in engineering, construction, management and technical support services. The company provides services across piling and foundations, bridges, jetties, landing aprons, equipment hiring and leasing, building works and road construction for the oil and gas industry and private-sector clients.

Our approach is built around modern, time-tested structural methods, intensive staff training and a corporate quality programme designed to support high-quality services and safe operations. Our team includes civil and structural engineers, designers, construction specialists and experienced project crews.
