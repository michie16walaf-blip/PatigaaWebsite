Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Patigaa Nigeria Limited ("Patigaa," "we," "us" or "our") handles information in connection with this website. It applies to visitors of this website only and does not cover information collected offline or through any other channel.

Information We Collect How We Use Information Cookies & Tracking Third-Party Links Data Security Data Retention Your Rights Children's Privacy Changes to This Policy Contact Us

1. Information We Collect

This website is primarily an informational, brochure-style site describing Patigaa Nigeria Limited's engineering, construction and marine infrastructure services. In its current form, this website does not use a live, server-connected contact form, so no personal information submitted through the contact page is transmitted to us or stored by us through the website itself.

When the contact form on this website is connected to a live backend or a secure form-handling service, the categories of information that visitors may voluntarily submit include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Company or organisation name
  • The subject and content of any message, enquiry or project request

We do not knowingly collect financial information, payment card details, or sensitive personal data (such as health, biometric or government identification information) through this website.

2. How We Use Information

This website's contact form is connected to a live backend, information voluntarily submitted through it would be used only for purposes such as:

  • Responding to enquiries, quotation requests and project discussions
  • Providing information about our services, equipment and project experience
  • Maintaining records of correspondence with prospective and existing clients
  • Complying with legal, regulatory or contractual obligations

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information submitted through this website to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This website does not currently use cookies, web beacons, analytics scripts or third-party advertising or tracking technologies. If this changes in the future — for example, if analytics software is added to help us understand how the website is used — this Privacy Policy will be updated beforehand to describe what is collected and how it is used, and, where required by law, your consent will be requested.

4. Third-Party Links

This website may include links to third-party websites, including social media platforms. Indicates that specific social media accounts have not yet been confirmed at the time of writing. Once live, those links will direct visitors away from this website to platforms operated by third parties, whose own privacy policies and terms will apply. Patigaa Nigeria Limited is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any third-party website.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information relating to this website and any information that may, in the future, be submitted through it. However, no method of transmission over the internet, and no method of electronic storage, can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Any information voluntarily submitted to Patigaa Nigeria Limited through this website would be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the relevant enquiry, to maintain business records, or to comply with applicable legal, tax or regulatory requirements, after which it would be securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights

Patigaa Nigeria Limited is committed to complying with applicable Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Subject to applicable law, individuals whose information we hold may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about them
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to any legal or contractual obligation to retain it
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of their personal information
  • Withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the "Contact Us" section below.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for businesses, government agencies and individuals seeking engineering, construction and related services, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including to reflect changes to the functionality of this website, such as the connection of a live contact form or the introduction of analytics tools. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last reviewed" date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
