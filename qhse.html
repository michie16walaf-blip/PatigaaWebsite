Quality, Safety & Environment

Safety, quality and environmental responsibility are integral to the way we plan and deliver our projects. Our goal is to protect people, property and the environment while delivering dependable engineering and construction services.

Quality

Quality Control & Assurance

Patigaa Nigeria Limited adopts a Total Quality Management approach. The company's quality system is described in company materials as “Excellence Through Change.” Quality teams and quality coordination roles have been established, while processes are defined, developed and documented.

The company's quality policy is supported by coordination from the Director of Operations and a Quality Assurance Programme (QAP) established to achieve the desired level of quality. The QAP applies across design, engineering, fabrication, testing and quality control, with regular top-management meetings held to review the programme.

Health & Safety

Health, Safety & Welfare

Patigaa Nigeria Limited states its commitment to maintaining high standards of health, safety and welfare for employees, visitors, the general public and host communities. Our policy is to comply with relevant legislation and industry best practices while conducting operations in a manner that does not harm people, property or the environment.

Environment

Environmental Responsibility

Operations are conducted in a manner intended to avoid harm to people, property and the environment, in line with relevant legislation and industry best practice, alongside a commitment to host-community development.

Management & Employee Responsibility

Safety Responsibilities

Responsibility for health, safety and welfare is defined at every level of the organisation.

Management Directors are ultimately responsible for the health and safety of employees and for providing adequate resources to meet and exceed current standards.

The Health, Safety & Environment Advisor coordinates a consistent approach to HS&E issues arising during company activities.

Managers and Heads of Departments ensure that employee health, safety and welfare roles are clearly defined, understood and implemented.

Engineers are responsible for planning and implementing health and safety requirements in project and construction activities.

Supervisors ensure that health, safety and welfare issues are highlighted, discussed and addressed.

Foremen and charge hands implement safety controls, promote safety and maintain effective communication with the workforce.

The workforce is expected to participate positively in health, safety and welfare requirements in line with corporate procedures and policies.

Continuous Improvement

Reviewed at Every Level, Every Project

Regular top-management meetings review the Quality Assurance Programme, supporting continuous improvement across design, engineering, fabrication, testing and quality control.
